Owner ask (2026-08-09, looking at About): _"things can be more thoughtfully
spaced here and we need to have significantly more pictures (like 3-5 per
section)."_

harvest plus a proposal. Assets and manifest: `scripts/stacks-photos.mjs`,
outputs in `public/images/stacks/`.

## Rejected, and why

- **Bodybuilding competition set** (Instagram 2022-10, 10 frames) — physique
  posing, already ruled out.
- **`2022/7 MVY/3 Chess`** (24 frames) — reads as a chess folder, is actually
  shirtless posing beside a lawn chess set.
- **AI-generated images** — the 2022-12 "generative AI is getting good" portrait
  set and a 2026 clover-on-a-book illustration. Not photographs, and the second
  one is visibly AI (the dice pips don't hold up).
- **Consensus promo cards** (~10) — marketing graphics with his face composited
  onto a Miami skyline. Not photographs.
- **Hackathon trophy shelf** (`1821229379982078352`) — cold blue lighting and
  it's an achievement display, which is the decorative-metadata register he cuts.
- **"Chappy's Book Notes" Notion screenshot** — the right _subject_ for Books,
  but a UI screenshot reads as a flat rectangle when printed into a 3D frame,
  and it exposes the database.
- **Mt Washington summit frames** — he isn't in the close ones and is
  unidentifiable in the group ones.
- **`2023/4 GAICo` event floor / `1822036735028814320`** — cold fluorescent
  office, and he isn't in them.
- **Tough Mudder / golf shots with burned-in text overlays** — kept the clean
  Tough Mudder frame, dropped the ones with caption bars baked into the pixels.
- **`dad-journal/Journal/images`** (165 scanned family photos) — genuinely good
  material for About, but it's his father's private journal. Not mine to publish
  without him saying so. Flagging it as a vein worth asking about.

## Payload

**837.0 KB total, 32 files** (was 494.7 KB / 15 files).

The 700 KB figure lives in `scripts/stacks-photos.mjs`'s own summary line, not
in `test-models.js` — that harness gates `/models/` only and has never asserted
on photos. I've moved the pipeline's printed budget to **1000 KB**, which leaves
~160 KB of headroom. If you'd rather have a real gate, 900 KB in a harness check
is the tighter number.

Two things kept it there. Caps are now assigned by **prop class rather than by
source** — pins 384px, polaroids and postcards 448px, framed and leaning prints
512–576px, all q72 — since nothing renders above roughly 500px on screen. And
five round-1 entries that were still on the 768px default (`gym-mirror`,
`beach-sunset`, `bros`, `postcard-budapest`, `talk-stanford`) were brought down
to their class, which paid for about 120 KB of the new set. Photos are
lazy-loaded after first paint.

The pipeline gained a `tw` source base and fractional-rect cropping
(`crop: {left, top, width, height}` in 0..1) for the two frames that needed it.
Re-running `node scripts/stacks-photos.mjs` reproduces every output byte for
byte; EXIF and ICC are stripped.
