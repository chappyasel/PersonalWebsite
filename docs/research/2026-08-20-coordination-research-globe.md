# Coordination Research globe

Status: implemented and revised through direct feedback.

## Intent

Add a small Coordination Research keepsake to the About shelf. It should feel
stranger and more alive than the neighboring metal marks without turning the
shelf into a science-fiction display.

## Boundaries

Do not redesign another shelf, change automatic scene-quality decisions, add
browser-only verification, or copy Coordination Research's canvas component
wholesale. Reuse its visual grammar with geometry that belongs in this scene.
The effect must remain render-target-free, and its diagnostics-off path must be
allocation- and frame-free.
