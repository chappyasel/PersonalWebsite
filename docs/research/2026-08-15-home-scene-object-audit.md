## How to read the ledger

Every row carries all required audit dimensions through compact profiles:

- `M-L/M/H/X`: movable with light/medium/heavy/massive handling. Fine pointer
  shows `grab`/`grabbing`; a 6 px move cancels activation. Mouse/pen may carry;
  touch raycasts at down, permits stationary activation, and never carries.
  Physics uses measured box/sphere collision where declared, gravity 3.4,
  shelf/floor/wall and neighbor contacts, clamped release, escape recovery,
  travel reset, and a per-unit-visit simulated/authored lock. Mass profiles
  are respectively λ/max-lift/throw-tilt: 22/.75/1, 18/.60/.8,
  13/.38/.45, 9/.20/.20.
- `D`: fixed Door, `pointer`, stationary activation only. The exact label and
  destination appear in the row; external labels alone gain `↗`. Keyboard and
  screen-reader access use the named natural link in the placard/flat page,
  not a duplicate hidden canvas tree.
- `E-skip/E-state`: fixed egg with pointer affordance. Motion is skipped or
  reduced to state change as named; replay/cancellation is local and travel
  returns authored state.
- `TF`: fixed Touch Focus target with no second-tap activation. `I`:
  inert/default pointer, no activation. `S`: subtle authored sway only.
- All profiles: inactive units ignore activation. Door Labels are fine-pointer
  only (350 ms first dwell, 100 ms switching), hide on drag/travel/out/panel/
  modal, and clamp to viewport/placard. On mobile, reduced-motion, Save-Data,
  or degraded render, the natural DOM/flat-page content remains the fallback;
  textures degrade through existing LOD and source geometry is omitted from
  flat mode.
- Lighting column `LD` means checked against cool sky fill and warm practical
  pools in both themes; highlights may be hot at fixture mouths but adjacent
  photographs, paper, wood, and vegetation retain texture. `risk` names the
  main support/contact/clip/occlusion issue.
- Provenance uses `A` (authored procedural geometry), `O` (owner artwork/photo,
  no third-party license), `C0` (CC0 manifest), `BY` (CC-BY manifest), and
  `MIT` (vendored open-source asset with its notice in the generated roster).
  Numbers are compressed KiB/triangles from the inspector. `atlas` means an
  existing ≤128 px shared themed atlas; `photo` uses the existing responsive
  image pipeline; `none` means no texture. Every row falls back to its natural
  DOM content or omission in flat/degraded mode.
