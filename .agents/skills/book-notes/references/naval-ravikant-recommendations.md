# Naval Ravikant recommendation research pattern

Session note from a book-recommendation task: Chappy asked for the top Naval website recommendations to consider, with more research than he would do himself.

## Primary sources checked

- `https://nav.al/read` – Naval's reading philosophy: read foundational/original books, read what you love until you love to read, examples include Adam Smith, Darwin, Watson/Crick, Feynman.
- `https://nav.al/love-read` – transcript emphasizing foundational texts and building a love of reading.
- `https://www.navalmanack.com/navals-recommended-reading` – main curated Navalmanack list, updated 2024.
