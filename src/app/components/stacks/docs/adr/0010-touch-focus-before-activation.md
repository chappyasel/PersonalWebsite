# Establish Touch Focus before activation

The first mobile tap on an Identity Prop will establish Touch Focus rather than immediately navigating or performing an explicit local action. It reveals a Portal Label when the prop has a destination or explicit local action, and gives movable-only props a persistent selected state without inventing a label. A subsequent tap on either an actionable focused prop or its now-actionable Portal Label follows the prop's semantic role through one shared activation path. The Portal Label appears adjacent to its prop and offset from the contact when space permits; otherwise it docks above the Peek Sheet with a short visual tether back to its owner. Touch Focus never expires on a timer; it clears when focus transfers or the visitor changes context through background touch, World travel, rail navigation, or Peek Sheet expansion. Touch visitors can therefore understand an outcome before committing and receive a dependable large target without forcing every object into identical behavior. Fine-pointer hover remains an immediate preview rather than a required first click.

Carrying is not an activation tap. Normal release, browser cancellation, and unexpected pointer-capture loss all clear the carried prop's prior Touch Focus. The next quick tap establishes fresh focus and runs Focus Lean against the prop's current position. Only the following tap may activate it.
