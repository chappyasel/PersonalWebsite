# Let the expanded Peek Sheet own touch

When the Peek Sheet expands for full reading, it enters Ambient Reading:
readable content owns touch until the sheet collapses. World travel, Touch
Focus, and carrying pause, while insects, lighting, and subtle scene motion
continue behind the sheet.

This prevents text scrolling from accidentally traveling through the World or
claiming a Movable Prop without making the scene feel frozen. It is chosen over
a fully interactive background, which creates competing gesture owners, and a
fully paused background, which breaks the homepage's sense of life.
