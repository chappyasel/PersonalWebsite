# Author mobile-specific Portrait Compositions

Each Unit will have an authored Portrait Composition rather than fitting its desktop arrangement into the space above the resident Peek Sheet. A narrow viewport cannot show the complete desktop shelf and keep its objects legible and touchable at the same time; mobile may therefore reposition or enlarge scene objects while preserving the Unit's identity and its role in the continuous World. Each stop owns its camera target and distance while all seven share a consistent lens and horizon language, allowing meaningful props to reach a dependable screen size without distorting the World through arbitrary scale. Identity Props must remain, while Set Dressing may be moved, simplified, replaced, or omitted; semantic parity takes precedence over exact inventory parity.
